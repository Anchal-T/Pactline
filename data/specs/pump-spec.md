## Mandatory Requirements

1. **Head at duty flow**: Head ≥ 35 m at 100 m³/h (±2% acceptance tolerance)
2. **Efficiency at duty point**: Wire-to-water efficiency ≥ 72%
3. **Motor compatibility**: 3-phase, 400V / 50Hz, IE3 minimum
4. **NPSH required**: NPSHr ≤ 4.5 m at duty flow (engineer-defined margin: +1.0 m)
5. **Connection**: DN100 flanged suction, DN80 flanged discharge (PN16)
6. **Fluid**: Water, 6–12°C, non-aggressive

## Optional Requirements

7. **Material**: Cast iron casing acceptable; stainless impeller preferred
8. **VFD-ready**: Motor suitable for variable-frequency drive operation
